IP knowledge

Patent Explorer

Explore the core concepts behind preliminary patentability analysis.

💡

Novelty

A claimed invention generally needs to be new relative to relevant prior art. A proper search is essential.

🧠

Inventive Step

The analysis asks whether the invention involves an inventive contribution rather than being an obvious development.

🔎

Prior Art

Earlier disclosures can affect patentability. Search patents, literature and other relevant disclosures.

Patent pathway

1

Define the invention

Identify technical features, claims and what is actually new.

2

Prior-art search

Search relevant patent and non-patent literature.

3

Patentability review

Consider novelty, inventive step and applicable exclusions/requirements.

4

Prepare application

Prepare claims, description, drawings where required and supporting documents.

5

Filing & prosecution

Follow the applicable office procedure and respond to examination requirements.

The explorer is educational. A professional patent search and legal assessment may still be necessary.
