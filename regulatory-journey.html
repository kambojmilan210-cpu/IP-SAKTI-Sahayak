Regulatory Journey

A visual journey from product idea to responsible commercialisation.

1

Identify the product

Capture formulation, ingredients, dosage form and intended use.

2

Classify the formulation

Map it to the relevant product category and applicable regulatory regime.

3

Check IP position

Review patent, trademark, GI, design, copyright and trade-secret options as applicable.

4

Check TK / ABS

Consider traditional knowledge and biological-resource access and benefit-sharing obligations where relevant.

5

Prepare evidence & documents

Collect formulation details, records, testing and other applicable documentation.

6

Regulatory submission / compliance

Follow the competent authority's current procedure and maintain compliance.

7

Responsible commercialisation

Launch only after completing applicable legal, regulatory and IP requirements.
